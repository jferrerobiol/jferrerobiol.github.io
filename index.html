I am an evolutionary biologist and ornithologist with a deep interest in the evolutionary history of species and in how species adapt to their environments. I am a lover of transdisciplinary science with a particular interest in studying complex traits that allow species to thrive in a changing world, such as migratory behaviour, by combining genomic and ecological data.

I am currently a postdoctoral research associate in the Bird Genoscape Project at Colorado State University, working on the genomics of local adaptation and migration in North American birds. Before that I was a postdoc at the University of Milan working on integrating genomic data with ecological data to evaluate the past, present and future effects of climate change on several bird species, and I did my PhD at the University of Barcelona using phylogenomics and population genomics to study the evolutionary history of shearwaters.
